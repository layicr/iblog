When the intro of "Tough Life" played, the lights dimmed and instantly lit up, and in that moment of ten thousand people singing in unison, all emotions were ignited. "Men KTV", "Love Song", "Good Person", "Kong" - classics played one after another, every lyric a mark etched in youth, melodies looped countless times in headphones finally resonating with thousands at the live show.

20 years since debut, Justin Lo's voice still pierces with power, his transition between chest and falsetto smooth as silk, stable as CD quality. His improvised adaptations and guitar solos showcase his singer-songwriter prowess. He brought a brand new creation "OldBoy", and also delivered the "lost gems" that longtime fans had been longing for - both nostalgic and freshly moving.

The most touching part is never the perfect stage, but the meaning of "Together Again COME TOGETHER": a two-way journey spanning 20 years, strangers embracing over the same melody, the regrets, stubbornness, and tenderness hidden in Cantonese songs finally finding resonance in this moment.
