Jolin Cai's "PLEASURE" World Tour Nanning Station presented a groundbreaking performance,

With no guest performers, no song requests, and no lengthy interactions—just 120 minutes of uninterrupted singing.


From the moment the prelude of "Pleasure" sounded, Jolin Tsai took control of the entire venue with pure musical energy.

She condensed her thirty-year music career into a flowing soundtrack: the power of "Dancer," the deep emotion of "Rewind," the confidence of "Ugly Beauty"...

Over thirty classic hits were seamlessly connected through meticulous arrangement, forming a complete "life soundtrack."

Jolin Cai proved: when the works are powerful enough, pure music itself is the most moving language.
