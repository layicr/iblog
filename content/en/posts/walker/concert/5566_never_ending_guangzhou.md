Keeping this appointment across time with 5566's "Never Ending" concert. As the prelude began, memories of chasing idol dramas and looping MP3s in my teenage years came flooding back. Tony Sun, Sam Wang, Jason Hsu and Rio Peng shared the stage, their chemistry intact, passionate and sincere under the lights.

Classics like "I Am Sad", "Long Time No See" and "Love Roaming" came one after another, with choruses rising and falling across the whole venue. Back then I only heard the joy and sorrow in the melody; years later, standing in the crowd, I finally understood the regret and perseverance hidden in the lyrics. The words "56 Will Never Die" are more than a slogan — they are the shared imprint of a generation's youth.

The lights will go out and the setlist will end, but tonight's cheers, choruses and emotions will not fade. This long-awaited reunion gave the passions of my youth a place to belong. Youth may drift away, but the story of 5566 never ends.
