In 2026, at the Spring Cocoon in Shenzhen, I finally made it to Jordan Chan's "BIG MAN 人一個" concert as I had wished. After all this long time, I finally broke free from the headphone loop and kept this date with my youth. The liveliness and emotion at the venue were even more healing and overwhelming than I had imagined. The whole performance was full of sincerity — Jordan Chan was on top of his game, with his aura maxed out. The moment the melodies of "Young and Dangerous" and "Invincible" kicked in, the whole crowd was instantly ignited, bringing back memories of that passionate, chivalrous world; "The One I Love", "Depend on Each Other" and "Exclusive Memory" were sung gently, full of the warmth of the years. The biggest surprise was the rearranged version of "Beginning to Understand" — a familiar melody reinterpreted anew, becoming a special easter egg of the night. When I was young, I only found the songs pleasant to listen to; it was only at the live show that I truly understood the regret and relief within the lyrics. Ten thousand people singing in chorus, a sea of light sticks flickering — it was the youthful resonance of a generation. The show will eventually come to an end, but this live memory will be treasured forever. Thank you, Jordan Chan, for keeping the appointment with your voice, fulfilling my youthful obsession, and making this night a uniquely unforgettable memory of my own.
