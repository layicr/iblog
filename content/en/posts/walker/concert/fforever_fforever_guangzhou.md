Traveling across time to keep this starry-sea rendezvous in Goat City, the melodies I had looped in my headphones for years were finally heard live under the night sky of the Olympic Sports Center. This night, we were all the glowing ones within the City of Stars.

Jerry Yan, Vanness Wu, Vic Zhou and Ashin shared the stage, and one classic hit after another rang out. "I Really Really Love You", "Cannot Lose You" and "Season of Fireworks" awakened memories buried deep in the heart; Mayday's "Party Animals" and "Love ing" ignited the passion of the whole audience. When the prelude of the finale "Meteor Shower" began, the entire venue turned into a vast sea of stars, and ten thousand people sang in unison, bringing tears to the eyes in an instant.

When young, listening to the songs I only understood the flutter of the heart; it was only after years, standing at the live show, that I truly read the tenderness and perseverance within the lyrics. The stage lights shifted and flowed, the singing passed through the sea of people, and old memories met new emotions in this very moment. This was not a mere concert, but a reunion gathering with youth after a long separation.

The music ends and people part, but the starlight remains in the heart. Tonight's singing, sea of lights and cheers will be treasured well. The stars are unforgotten — Forever Forever
