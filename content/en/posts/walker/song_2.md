At that time in Hengchun, I kept looping the song "Ballad" on repeat. At Hong Chai Keng, I wanted to lose myself in the scene. When I crouched at the harbor, gazing at the sea, the scarecrow had been my only companion along the west coastline—but what I wanted to wait for was a dog...
