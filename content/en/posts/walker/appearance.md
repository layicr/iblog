Lately I've been listening to Stefanie Sun's songs,
Different eras, the same voice,
When I heard "The Way You Look",
Remembering a book I read long ago,
It feels so much like youth,
That utterly insignificant look...


In the future, if you have a favorite singer, find a way to go to his concert and be with those who love him. You never know how long that singer will stay famous, nor how long he'll choose to live. You can only, while he's still here, make him a part of your memories.

------From "Accompanying Anthony: A Long Journey"
