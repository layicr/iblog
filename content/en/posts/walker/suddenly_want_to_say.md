Will you suddenly appear,

At a coffee shop around a corner of some airport,

Your shoulder on my left, a smile on my right,

Coming and going,

One, two, three, four,

I think,

A laugh, a glance,

A simple embrace can carry us to old age.

Then a matcha latte, please—hot.

Pick a spot, put on the earphones,

Sipping a coffee while eating two shaomai,

With the boiling facets of life, hopes and changes to come,

Suddenly want to say,

No coffee can compare to you.

END
