After overtime on Saturday and Sunday,

Then walked into an unremarkable shop by the roadside,

Said HI to the boss,

Order a set meal,

Get a bottle of soy milk,

Sitting right next to the speakers,

And this time it's Mayday's song again,

I've always thought of myself as,

Not a proper Mayday fan,

Though I've watched a concert at the Xi'an Stadium,

Unlike the lovely people around me who sing along from the first song to the last,

Just sitting quietly and listening,

Ten thousand people singing along to "Love ing",

The tenderness of holding up phones as glow sticks,

Following the encore, the last song,

In that high summer others can't touch,

Those gentle moments,

Please don't disturb,

Pulling a bottle of RIO out of my bag,

Drink it up, the rain stops,

With the lingering scent of wine on me,

While crossing the road,

A sudden gust of wind blows by,

The whole world is clear,

In a blink, only I remain.


END


2020/08/08


Nanning Wuxu International Airport
