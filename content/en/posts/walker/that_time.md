Those times,


Gone and never returned,


Look back once more,


Only savoring remains,


Savor it once more,


Only sighs remain,


Sigh once more,


Only letting go remains,


Let it go once more,


Only moving forward remains,


Move forward again,


Only the present remains,


In the present moment once more,


Only cherishing remains,


Cherish once more,


Only the present, right here and now, remains.
