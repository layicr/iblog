Suddenly realized,


In a few days I'm going to sing to Wu Bai,


I haven't practiced a single song,


Right now my heart's in a total panic,


No way, it can't be like this,


Cut 30 minutes of overtime every day,


And again squatting in the lobby to rehearse,


Passion moves into action,


Get the little stools ready,


Which song to start with?


"Last Dance", lost on the B-side of a cassette?


Or "The Tie", the one I thought would catch fire but never did?


Beijing . /Beijing
