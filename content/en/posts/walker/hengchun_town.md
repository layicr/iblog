At that time in Hengchun,

Keep looping the song "Ballad" on repeat,

At Hong Chai Keng, I wanted to lose myself in the scene,

When I crouch at the harbor,

Gazing at the sea,

Along the west coastline, my only companion has been the scarecrow,

But what I want to wait for is a dog...
