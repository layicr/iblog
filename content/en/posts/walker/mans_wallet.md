Finished the TV drama "Blossoms Shanghai" over the Spring Festival,


In it, Uncle Yeshu says:


A man has three wallets,


First, how much money you actually have;


Second, your credit;


Third, how much money others think you have;


Whether in business,


Or in life,


Guard these three wallets,


Then you're set.
