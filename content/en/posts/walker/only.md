When listening to Accusefive's "The Only One",

Stop everything in my hands,

Started the endless single-track loop,

Sitting quietly,

With an added calmness,

A thing like time,

Really,

When it's the best version of us,

Staying only at the best moment,

The boundaries of happiness?

emmm~

Haven't we all been living seriously?

Handing myself over to the busy rush,

What you get is a sense of security—all bullshit.

That year he said,

Slow down your own pace,

Are you and I willing to measure this world?

Suddenly want a matcha latte,

Turned on the SONY NWA55,

Downloading in Hi-Res format,

Put on the AKG,

Maybe I'll write down the lyrics.

END


Zhejiang . Huzhou . /Huzhou
