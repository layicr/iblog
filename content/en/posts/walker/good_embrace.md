Pick a corner of the sofa, set down a little Coke,


Had meant to hold it and read a bit,


But the moment I see the little one in my arms,


Her eyes so hazy and dreamy,


A smile is innocent, free of any impurity,


Truly tugs at the heart,


Just like that,


You look at me, I look at you,


As if we've all gotten used to this warmth,
