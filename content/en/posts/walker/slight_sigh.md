Stayed quietly in a bookstore in the city for a long time,


Got my fill of fresh air in the mountain village,


Wake up—back to real life,


The pen drew a few strokes, and I let out a soft sigh,


True,


How could I understand now...


Taiwan . Chishang, Taitung . /Chrshang
