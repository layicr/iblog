I said to the fish,


Come on,


Come ashore,


Quit your job underwater,


Elevating myself through travel,


Bidding farewell to the filth in the water,


Let the sky purify your soul,


The fish said to me,


If I'd believed your chicken soup for the soul,


Tonight I'll turn into fish soup.
