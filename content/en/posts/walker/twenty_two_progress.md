That year,
In Tainan, I took a cab to the night market,
On the road,
The driver kept driving in circles,
The music keeps looping "Life's a Struggle",
Not wanting to think, just staring out the window,
Getting off the bus,
Every late night, I learn the next song.


That year,
Just about to hit thirty,
I really love "Twenty-Two",


When dreams beckon to me once again,


An intersection not yet marked on the map,

Is the very start of the story,

Hmm? Hmm.

And so the blog gained a category: Two-Two Into Three,

There's more in the tea than just the tea's aroma,

As for me, at most I've got swagger...

Taiwan . Tainan . /Tainan
