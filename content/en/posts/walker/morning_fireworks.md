Early in the morning before dawn,

Sitting outside the window with a heater, feeling the breeze,

Zoned out for a while,

Autumn is still romantic,

The wind rustles the leaves outside the window.


The morning bustle,

I shouldered my luggage,

Those who follow along with the crowd,

Hmm? Don't know what I'm rushing about?

Panicked for a moment,

Bought a hot matcha latte by the roadside,

Holding a latte, I start walking again, warming my hands.

Stirred by the wind, roadside fallen leaves rise,

That must be the mark of our youth.

"Boarding now for train Gxxxx from XX to XX..."

Earphones on, and it starts playing,

"Give me your smiling lips and eyes, give me your unrefined innocence and freedom,"

END

Jiangsu . Nanjing South Station /NanjingnanStation
