Yellowed photographs,


Time spins,


I'd forgotten I was once a backpacker,


I had imagined saving up enough private money in these years,


With packs on my back, I tread the southern skies; camera in hand, off to Antarctica,


Moved in silence before the high mountains and flowing water,


Hmm? Hmm! NICE!
