Following only a calm heart,

And a pair of slightly weary feet,

Every day shuttling between two points in this charming city,

Go order milk tea without sugar,

Go to FamilyMart for two pieces of pastry,

Watching people standing in line at the hospital,

Watching the beautiful lights at the intersection,

After overtime,

Came back and rushed to class again,

Finish this section, move on to the next,

It's always that sense of distance,

Shook out my trench coat,

Want some Japanese food,

Said hi to the boss,

Sneakily pulled a bottle of RIO out of my bag,

A night carrying a chill,

Gazing along the roadside,

This city's streets are quiet and desolate,

A song starts playing in my earphones,

Feeling,

At the age for going to Wu Bai's concerts,

Those nights of wandering alone,

This moment, this season,

Telling of time that never fades...


END
