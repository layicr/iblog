I know the bragging we did,

Will be laughed away with the wind,

Of reality，

Climbed to Lake Yamdrok... No wait—climbed to Lhamo La Tso at 5,300 meters,

Seen the fairyland of Kanas Lake,

Packed up the sand of Shapotou in Zhongwei,

One person, one piece of fried chicken each, on a footbridge in Beijing, Shanghai, or Guangzhou,


Tibet . Xinjiang . Ningxia . /Tibet
