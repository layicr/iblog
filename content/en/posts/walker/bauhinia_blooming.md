Shuttling through three cities these past two days,


Ten stops across subway, train and intercity rail,


Slept less than seven hours in total,


Worked like a madman all day today,


Just got back and settled down, meant to type a few words,


Only then did I realize my body was asking for some rest,


Fingertips resting on the keyboard,


But the cursor keeps blinking there,


Alright,


No inspiration, no touch either,


Alright,


This trip needs time to settle and settle again...


First, upload a song, "Bauhinia in Full Bloom",


I like this song very much too,


Is a work honoring the 25th anniversary of HK's return to the motherland,


For HK,


Over the past decade-plus I've been to many major cities, yet I've never set foot in HK,


Walking into someone else's world requires an attitude,


So, so,


Let's listen to some music first...


Guangdong . Qingyuan . /Qingyuan
