These years have been a busy life,


Stealing a moment to write a few words and take some photos,


Just wanting to publish a photo-text collection of my own,


Simple, genuine, no sneakiness,


An impromptu short piece, with photos from these past years attached.


It's actually hard to realize,


The server moved through several countries,


My blog has been taken down several times in the last three years,


Hmm?


Counting, it's over eight times already,


How to put it,


Are words and photos too lean?


There's no so-called original intention,


Maybe I just want to have a romance with sentiment,


Sometimes you should stop in your tracks,


Say sorry, laugh it off,


At ease, going with the flow.


No matter how others show their disdain,


Free-spirited and footloose, owing nothing,


Living up to every encounter, that's me,


A psycho.


END


Shanghai
