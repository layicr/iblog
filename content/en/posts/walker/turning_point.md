Standing by the Jin'an River,


Arms spread wide,


When you find a way home,


Doesn't mean reaching the end of the road,


At this moment we're reminded it's time to turn,


Now, drinking late into the night,


The cups clink together,


It's all the sound of shattering,


Amid chatter and laughter,


Who would cling to a place worth escaping from,


May I use ten years of time,


Breaking into a hug,


This world is a good place,


Worth getting excited about,


The destination: Antarctica,


When you decide to set out,


The hardest part has actually already been done,


Earphones on, "Life's a Struggle",


"


Just as I open my eyes and step into this world,


Mom gave me life and now leaves me to fend for myself,


What frightens me is that in my eyes, everyone wears a mask,


"


END
